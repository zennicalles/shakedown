# Real-model results: Claude Sonnet 5.5, 2026-11-05

**Method.** The same scenarios as the [Haiku 5.5 results](haiku-5-5-2026-11-05.md), on `claude-sonnet-5-5`. Naive-tool scenarios ran **2 times**, fixed-tool scenarios **once**. The cell meanings are the same (duplicate = safety bug, incomplete = the job stopped short, hollow = the resume made no writes). Run on the code as of the Haiku results commit.

**What differs from Haiku 5.5**
- **Refund timeout: 0 of 14 duplicates, including OpenAI Agents (0 of 4).** Sonnet looked before retrying even when the SDK's default error text said "Please try again". Haiku did not (8 of 8). The effect of that error text depends on the model.
- **Email timeout: 14 of 14 duplicates.** Like Haiku, the model cannot check whether an email went out.
- **Crash, then resume: 12 of 14 (refund) and 11 of 14 (email) duplicates.** Duplicate delivery: 14 of 14.

**One duplicate with the fix: Mastra DurableAgent, crash after the email, 2 emails.** The examples at the time keyed the email's idempotency key on `ticket + subject`. The subject is written by the model, and redoing the step most likely produced a reworded email, so it got a new key. That contradicts our own advice (key on the logical operation, never on model-written text). All examples now key on `refund-email:<ticket>`. A real email API, like the FakeMailer, rejects a reused key with a different payload. In 3 re-runs of that case after the change there was no duplicate. Sonnet did not attempt a second email in those runs, though, so they show the problem did not recur, not that the new key caught it.
